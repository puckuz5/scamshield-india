## Lottery and prize scams
You "won" a lottery, a car or a KBC prize you never entered. To claim it you must pay a processing fee, tax or GST, or share bank details. RBI's BE(A)WARE booklet lists lottery fraud as a common method. Real prizes don't require you to pay first.

## Fake loan apps
Ads or messages offer instant loans without CIBIL checks, then charge "processing fees" upfront or use app permissions to harass contacts. RBI advises verifying the lender (it should be a regulated bank or NBFC) and its app before engaging.

## Friend / family / social-media impersonation
A message from a "new number" or a cloned social-media profile claims to be a relative or friend in an emergency and asks for money urgently. Attackers can now clone voices with AI, so even a familiar-sounding voice note or call is not proof. RBI's booklet lists social-media impersonation; the 2026 Quick Heal report highlights AI voice cloning.

## What to do
Never pay to receive a prize. Verify lenders before sharing documents. Call the person back on their **old, saved number** before sending money. If money was lost, call **1930** or file at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
