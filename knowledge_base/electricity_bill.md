## How it works
A message says your power will be disconnected tonight because last month's bill "was not updated". It asks you to call or WhatsApp an "electricity officer" on a personal number. The caller then sends a payment link or asks you to install an app to "update" the bill, and takes over your account or collects money.

## Red flags (from discom advisories such as Tata Power-DDL)
- Disconnection warnings from a personal mobile number instead of the company's sender ID
- Being asked to call or WhatsApp an individual "officer"
- Payment links or third-party apps to "update" the bill
- Payment to a private account or individual

## What to do
Check your bill only on your electricity company's official website, app or toll-free number. Pay only through authorised channels. Don't call numbers from the message. Report the number on **Chakshu**; if money was lost, call **1930**.
