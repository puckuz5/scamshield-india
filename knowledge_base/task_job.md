## How it works
Students and homemakers get a WhatsApp, Telegram or SMS offer of easy work from home: liking YouTube videos, rating hotels or products, or following accounts. The first few tasks pay small amounts to build trust. Then the victim is moved to "prepaid" or "merchant" tasks that need a deposit, with promises of a bigger payout. The deposits keep growing and the money never comes back. Fake job portals also ask for "registration fees" or bank details.

## Red flags
- Earn ₹2,000–₹10,000 a day for simple online tasks
- Contact from an unknown number, moved to Telegram
- Small payouts first, then a deposit is required to "unlock" earnings
- Registration, training or security fees for a job
- No interview, no company verification

## What to do
Real employers do not charge you to work. Stop paying, don't "recover" losses by paying more, and report it. If money was lost, call **1930** or file at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
