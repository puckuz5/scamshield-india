## How it works
Victims are added to WhatsApp or Telegram groups that share "VIP" stock tips, IPO allotments or crypto signals. Group "experts" and fake members post screenshots of huge profits. Victims are sent a link to a trading app that is not on the official app store. The app shows growing (fake) profits to encourage bigger deposits. When the victim tries to withdraw, the account is blocked, or they are told to pay "tax", "fees" or "verification charges" first.

## Red flags (from I4C's advisory)
- Unsolicited investment advice in groups you didn't ask to join
- Trading apps shared through private links instead of official app stores
- Guaranteed returns or "VIP" / insider tips
- Having to pay more money before you can withdraw
- Brokers or platforms you cannot verify

## What to do
Don't act on tips from unknown groups. Install trading apps only from official app stores and check that the broker is registered. Leave the group and report it. If money was lost, call **1930** or file at **cybercrime.gov.in** quickly.
