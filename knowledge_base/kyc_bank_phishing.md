## How it works
An SMS, email or WhatsApp message says your bank account, card, PAN or wallet will be blocked unless you update KYC, link PAN or verify details "today". The link opens a fake site that looks like your bank. Anything you type there, including passwords, card numbers, CVV and OTPs, goes to the fraudster. In voice-phishing (vishing), a caller posing as a bank official asks for these details directly or for an OTP "sent by mistake".

RBI's BE(A)WARE booklet lists phishing links, vishing, OTP fraud and SIM-swap among the most common ways people lose money.

## Red flags
- Account / card / PAN "will be blocked" with a deadline
- A link that isn't your bank's official domain, or a shortened link
- Anyone asking for an OTP, CVV, PIN, password or card number
- Sender is a normal 10-digit mobile number instead of a bank sender ID

## What to do
Banks don't ask for OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords. Don't click the link; open your bank's official app or website yourself. If your phone suddenly loses network, contact your operator and bank (possible SIM swap). If money was lost, call **1930** and your bank.
