## How it works
A message contains a link or file ending in **.apk**, dressed up as a traffic challan, a government scheme, a wedding invitation, a bank "update" or a delivery app. Installing it lets malware read SMS, steal OTPs and take over banking apps. In the fake customer-care version, the victim searches online for a helpline, reaches a fraudster, and is asked to install a screen-sharing or remote-access app so the "agent" can fix a refund. The fraudster then watches or controls the phone.

## Red flags
- Any .apk file or link from SMS, WhatsApp or email
- "Install this app to get your refund / pay your challan / update KYC"
- Requests to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport or any screen-sharing app
- Helpline numbers found in search results or social media comments rather than the official website

## What to do
Install apps only from the official app store. Never install apps or share your screen at a stranger's request. Use only numbers from the official website or your bank statement. If you installed something, turn on airplane mode, contact your bank, and call **1930**.
