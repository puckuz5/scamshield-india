## How it works
A recorded or live call claims to be from TRAI, the Department of Telecom or your mobile operator. It says your number will be cut off within hours because of "illegal activity" or incomplete KYC, and asks you to press a key or talk to an "officer". The call is often handed over to fake police, which turns it into a digital-arrest scam. Variants include forged TRAI letters and offers of high rent for installing a mobile tower in exchange for a "registration fee".

## The fact that breaks the scam
TRAI's own advisory says it does not investigate individual consumers, does not ask for Aadhaar, bank details or OTPs, does not contact customers about disconnecting their number (directly or through agents), and does not collect money or run investigations over phone, messaging or video calls.

## Red flags
- "Your SIM / mobile number will be blocked in 2 hours"
- Press 9 (or any key) to speak to an officer
- Call is transferred to "police" or "cyber crime"
- Request for Aadhaar, OTP or a payment to keep your number active
- Mobile tower installation offers that need an upfront fee

## What to do
Disconnect the call. Never share personal or banking details. Flag the number on **Chakshu** (Sanchar Saathi portal) or the TRAI DND app. If money was lost, call **1930** or report at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
