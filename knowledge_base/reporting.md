## If you LOST money or were a victim
- Call **1930**, the national cybercrime helpline, as fast as possible. Quick reporting helps banks freeze money before it moves through mule accounts. The 2026 Quick Heal report says more than ₹11,000 crore has been saved through the 1930 helpline and the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting system.
- File a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in** (National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal).
- Inform your bank and block cards / UPI.

## If you only RECEIVED a suspicious call, SMS or WhatsApp
- Report it on **Chakshu**, a facility on the Department of Telecommunications' **Sanchar Saathi** portal. Chakshu is for reporting suspected fraud communications. It is **not** for reporting money already lost; for that, use 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.
